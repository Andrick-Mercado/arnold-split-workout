How to use this split: Train the three days in order (e.g. Mon / Tue / Wed or with rest days between). The classic Arnold split runs each session twice per week for 6 training days. Start with the volume below and adjust based on recovery. Focus on progressive overload — add weight or reps when you hit the top of the range with good form.

Day 1 — Chest & Back

Barbell Bench Press

4 sets × 8-12 reps
Equipment: Flat Bench + Barbell

Lower the bar to mid-chest, elbows ~45°, drive feet into the floor and press up explosively. Control the descent.
