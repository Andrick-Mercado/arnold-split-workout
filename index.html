ARNOLD SPLIT

Classic 3-Day Bodybuilding Routine
Chest & Back • Shoulders & Arms • Legs
Animated form demos

Day 1: Chest & Back Day 2: Shoulders & Arms Day 3: Legs
How to use this split: Train the three days in order (e.g. Mon / Tue / Wed or with rest days between). The classic Arnold split runs each session twice per week for 6 training days. Start with the volume below and adjust based on recovery. Focus on progressive overload — add weight or reps when you hit the top of the range with good form.

Day 1 — Chest & Back

Barbell Bench Press

4 sets × 8-12 reps
Equipment: Flat Bench + Barbell

Lower the bar to mid-chest, elbows ~45°, drive feet into the floor and press up explosively. Control the descent.

Day 2 — Shoulders & Arms

Barbell Overhead Press

4 sets × 8-12 reps
Equipment: Barbell + Bench or Power Rack (standard at 24 Hour Fitness)

Bar starts at upper chest. Press straight up to lockout. Brace core. Seated or standing both work. Machine shoulder press is a fine alternative if preferred.

Dumbbell Lateral Raises

4 sets × 10-15 reps
Equipment: Dumbbells (full rack at every 24 Hour Fitness)

Slight bend in elbows. Raise arms out to the sides until parallel to floor. Lead with elbows, control the negative. Lateral raise machine is also available at most locations.

3 sets × 12-15 reps
Equipment: Cable machine (rope attachment) or Dumbbells — both standard

Day 3 — Legs

Barbell Back Squat

4 sets × 8-12 reps
Equipment: Squat Rack + Barbell

Bar on upper traps. Brace hard, sit back and down to at least parallel. Drive through heels, knees tracking over toes.
